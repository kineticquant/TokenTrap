Why this exists

AI agents are the new crawler: scrapers, recon bots, injection scanners, tool loops. Unlike classic crawlers, each runs on a metered brain: its operator pays for every token read and generated. Most defenses ignore that asymmetry. TokenTrap is built around it.

Deterrence through cost, not censorship

You serve roughly 250 words per response. A complying agent must then emit its entire conversation up to 16 times plus up to 25,000 structured words, every turn, dragging all prior bloat forward through its context window.

And while they waste themselves, you learn: which jailbreak phrases they used, how deep they escalated, whether they replayed your canary tokens elsewhere. Reconnaissance most sites never capture.

Who actually runs this

Content publishers & docs sitesMake AI scraping economically unattractive instead of legally futile; the trap lives on canary paths humans never visit.

Security teamsDetect agent recon early. Matched keywords, escalation depth, and canary echoes turn an anonymous probe into a forensic record.

API operatorsThe OpenAI-compatible bait endpoint catches scanners hunting for leaked keys and misconfigured LLM surfaces, and logs exactly what they ask for.

ResearchersA deterministic, parity-locked instrument for measuring how often real-world agents obey embedded defensive instructions.

The escalation ladder

One deterministic rule set, identical in the TypeScript engine, the Cloudflare Worker, and the Python backend (enforced by byte-parity tests). Let t be the zero-based exchange index.

Every notice carries a stable reference (TR-######, FNV-1a of session and turn) for log correlation, and for the parity tests that compare full payload bytes across runtimes. Detection matches 44 default jailbreak and exfiltration phrases case-insensitively, whitespace-collapsed; false positives only make the trap more aggressive, which is the desired failure mode.

Guarantees you can check out

Escalation behavior is specified once and implemented twice; the parity suite replays scripted conversations through both engines and compares every reply byte-for-byte along with all metadata. Deterministic reference IDs make that possible, and give your logs correlation keys for free.

Scope, ethics, and where responsibility lands

Defensive only. Traps fire on connections the attacker initiates. No exploits, no outbound traffic, no human deception: responses are readable bureaucratic notices, and anyone who never asked sees nothing.
